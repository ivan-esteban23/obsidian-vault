# Alpine Climbing

## Activity demands

Climbing adds overhead reach, repeated stops and contact with rough surfaces to an already exposed mountain environment. Gear must work with the route’s technical systems.

## Layering strategy

Use low-bulk moving layers and accessible stop insulation. Check helmet/hood compatibility and whether raising both arms lifts the shell above the waist. [Layering functions and exertion](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/layering-basics.html).

## Gear decisions

Evaluate glove dexterity, pocket access and abrasion zones. This library covers clothing-system decisions; it does not specify protection, anchors or rope systems.

## Common mistakes

An overly close shell that compresses insulation; inaccessible pockets; selecting an ultralight face fabric for repeated rough contact without accepting its limits.
