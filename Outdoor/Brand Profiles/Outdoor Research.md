# Outdoor Research

**Country:** United States · **Origin:** United States · **Region:** American · **Cost:** Premium. Country identifies the brand base used here, not where every product is manufactured.

## Brand philosophy

Problem-solving mountain clothing and accessories emphasize movement, exposure and protection during changing conditions. [Outdoor Research — design / technical evidence](https://www.outdoorresearch.com/blogs/stories/the-science-behind-ascentshell-technology).

## Strengths

A strong shortlist for gloves, weather accessories and breathable shells. AscentShell documents a different construction approach from a conventional non-air-permeable shell.

## Weaknesses and tradeoffs

No waterproof fabric removes the need to vent during hard effort. The Infinite Guarantee has exclusions, including normal wear and user-created damage. These are editorial selection tradeoffs, not measured defect rates.
