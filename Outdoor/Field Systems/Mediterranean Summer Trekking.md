# Mediterranean Summer Trekking

For central Chile’s dry-summer trekking context, particularly routes beginning in warm foothills and reaching more exposed elevation. Summer here is December–February.

## Functional kit

| Phase | Functions to cover |
| --- | --- |
| During hot effort | Light breathable sun coverage, ventilated legwear and footwear suited to the terrain. |
| Exposed or cooling terrain | Add wind protection and appropriate insulation as conditions change. |
| Rain or overnight reserve | Carry the storm and sleeping protection justified by route, forecast and consequences of delay. |

## Field sequence

1. Check source reliability and carry capacity for the longest plausible dry section.
2. Avoid wearing heavy insulation merely because the route eventually enters the mountains.
3. Keep eye, skin and head protection appropriate to the exposure; review the timing of exposed sections.
4. Reassess the kit if the route changes from foothill trekking to snow or technical alpine terrain.

## Failure to avoid

Dry seasonal averages do not establish the weather on a particular trip. Northern Chile’s Altiplano has a different precipitation pattern; this system is not a universal desert calendar.
