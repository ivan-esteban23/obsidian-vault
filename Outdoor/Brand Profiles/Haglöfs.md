# Haglöfs

**Country:** Sweden · **Origin:** Sweden · **Region:** Scandinavian · **Cost:** Premium. Country identifies the brand base used here, not where every product is manufactured.

## Brand philosophy

Outdoor clothing combines Swedish wet-weather needs with activity-dependent waterproof and insulating systems. [Haglöfs — design / technical evidence](https://www.haglofs.com/en/alper-proof).

## Strengths

PROOF variants distinguish everyday rain protection from higher-output or longer wet outings. The distinction is more useful than treating all Haglöfs shells as identical.

## Weaknesses and tradeoffs

A shared technology name does not establish equal performance across variants. Older stock and current disclosure language can differ. These are editorial selection tradeoffs, not measured defect rates.
