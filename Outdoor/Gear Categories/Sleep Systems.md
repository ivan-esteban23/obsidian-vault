# Sleep Systems

**Function:** Keep the sleeper insulated and dry through a combination of bag or quilt, mat, clothing and shelter.

## Selection criteria

Compare comfort and limit ratings carefully. Choose bag dimensions that permit loft without unnecessary empty space and pair it with a suitable insulated mat. A rating is a standardized reference, not a personal guarantee. [Backpacking sleeping bags](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/sleeping-bag-backpacking.html).

## Use and field checks

Keep sleeping insulation in its own dry barrier. Air it when possible and store it uncompressed between trips according to care instructions.

## Failure modes and tradeoffs

A warm bag cannot fully compensate for a poorly insulated mat or wet fill; an extreme rating is not a comfort target.
