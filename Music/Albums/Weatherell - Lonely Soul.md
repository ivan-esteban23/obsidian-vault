# Weatherell — Lonely Soul - LP

![[attachments/Alternative 2026/14-weatherell.jpg|320]]

**18 September 2026** · Album · Grunge / shoegaze / alternative rock / electronica · Artist Bandcamp edition

Toronto multi-instrumentalist Sean Hackl makes a debut concerned with grief after the death of a close friend. The artist describes a maximalist mix of heavy guitars, substantial beats, bass and synthesizers. Candy Eyes sits early in a record that brings grunge and shoegaze into an electronic setting.

**First spin:** Candy Eyes.
