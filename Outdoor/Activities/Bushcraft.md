# Bushcraft

## Activity demands

Camp-oriented outdoor skills place a premium on abrasion resistance, repair and comfortable low-output work. Legal land use and low-impact practice determine what activities are appropriate.

## Layering strategy

Use sturdy legwear and adaptable insulation rather than a high-output outfit alone. Keep an independent rain layer when durable woven clothing is only water-resistant. [Layering functions and exertion](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/layering-basics.html).

## Gear decisions

Favor maintainable footwear and equipment. In Torres del Paine, use designated camping and cooking arrangements; do not import fire-lighting, vegetation cutting or shelter-building assumptions.

## Common mistakes

Confusing waxed cotton blends with waterproof fabric; prioritizing camp projects over land rules; exposing delicate synthetics or down to heat.
