# Pack Waterproofing

**Function:** Maintain an independent dry boundary around sleeping insulation, spare clothing and vulnerable electronics.

## Selection criteria

Use a suitable liner or dry bags, organize wet and dry items separately, and understand the closure limits. A rain cover can be supplementary protection but leaves openings and the harness side exposed. [Backpacking in rain](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/backpacking-in-the-rain.html).

## Use and field checks

Pack the sleep reserve first into its protected space. Keep the shell accessible outside that reserve and check closures after every camp move.

## Failure modes and tradeoffs

A coated backpack or a fabric labeled water-resistant does not eliminate leakage through seams or openings.
