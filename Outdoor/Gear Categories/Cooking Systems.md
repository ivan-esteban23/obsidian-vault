# Cooking Systems

**Function:** Provide a compatible stove, fuel, pot and permitted cooking arrangement for the route.

## Selection criteria

Check fuel availability, stove operating limits and permitted use before departure. Wind and cold affect performance; do not assume one fuel or stove behaves the same in every environment. [Backpacking stove selection](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/backpacking-stove.html).

## Use and field checks

Practice outdoors in a safe location and follow manufacturer clearance and windscreen instructions. In Torres del Paine, confirm designated cooking arrangements with current official information.

## Failure modes and tradeoffs

Do not cook inside a tent or enclosed shelter. Improvised windscreens can overheat some canister systems; a manufacturer-approved arrangement matters.
