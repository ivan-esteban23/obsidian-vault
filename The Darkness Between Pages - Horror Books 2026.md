# The Darkness Between Pages

*A horror reading guide / 2026*

**30 books. Five routes into the dark.**

From haunted houses and folk nightmares to body horror and strange futures, this guide brings together 2026 releases highlighted by horror critics and readers. It is a curated selection, not a definitive ranking; the numbers are for navigation.

**RESEARCH CUTOFF:** 11 November 2026. Publication years refer to the books, not a later reissue. Goodreads popularity and critic selections are different signals; the source links show the basis for each recommendation.

> [!note] Start here
> **For a haunting:** The Caretaker. **For Gothic atmosphere:** Japanese Gothic. **For wilderness dread:** Monumental. **For a strange future:** Dead but Dreaming of Electric Sheep.
