**9 January 2026** · Album · Experimental rock / post-punk / no-wave elements · 4AD

Dry Cleaning develops its third album through rehearsals and sessions in London, Chicago, Dublin and France, with Cate Le Bon producing. The release notes place spoken-word delivery beside punk, no-wave and pastoral guitar influences. Hit My Head All Day introduces its eleven-track original edition.
