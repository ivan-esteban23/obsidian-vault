# Ski Touring

## Activity demands

Long heat-producing climbs followed by colder transitions and descents create a strong ventilation problem. Snow and avalanche terrain also change the required skills.

## Layering strategy

Wear less uphill, ventilate early and protect insulation before transitions. Keep gloves and a wind/storm layer accessible without emptying the pack. [Layering functions and exertion](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/layering-basics.html).

## Gear decisions

Choose a pack and clothing that work with the actual touring equipment. Avalanche education and dedicated rescue equipment are outside this collection’s detailed kit scope.

## Common mistakes

Using resort insulation for a sustained climb; sweating through the only gloves; assuming a clothing checklist is an avalanche plan.
