# Patagonia Region

Patagonia is a broad geographic region, not one weather zone. Western wet and maritime influences, sheltered terrain, mountains and drier eastern environments can require different equipment on neighboring itineraries. [Torres del Paine official 2026 recommendations](https://parquetorresdelpaine.cl/recomendaciones-para-su-viaje-2026/).

## Planning context

Build from the actual route and season. A windy coastal or mountain trek may combine wet cold, maritime exposure and alpine passages; the regional name alone does not justify expedition-weight clothing or guarantee rain every day.

## Clothing and layering

Use a light moving outfit with accessible wind and rain protection. Keep the static and sleep reserves dry. Manage cuffs, hood and loose equipment before reaching exposed ground; if the wind compromises safe movement, changing plans matters more than a stronger fabric.

## Multi-day equipment priorities

For self-supported travel, prioritize shelter architecture, permitted campsite choice, load compatibility and dry storage before fine differences between expensive jackets. Food and water plans must follow the actual itinerary rather than a regional packing stereotype.
