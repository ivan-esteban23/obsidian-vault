# Mountaineering

## Activity demands

Mountain travel can combine long approaches, snow, wind and sustained cold stops. Route technique, acclimatization and equipment compatibility require preparation beyond clothing selection.

## Layering strategy

Manage sweat on the approach and retain a substantial stop layer. Test hood and glove operation with the equipment used on the route. [Layering functions and exertion](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/layering-basics.html).

## Gear decisions

Match boots and protective clothing to the objective. A trekking boot or a general waterproof jacket is not automatically compatible with technical snow or ice travel.

## Common mistakes

Buying expedition insulation without learning moisture management; ignoring reach or footwear compatibility; treating a brand rating as a safety certification.
