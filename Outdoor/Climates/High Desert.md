# High Desert

**Planning context:** Aridity combines with elevation, solar exposure and potentially large day-to-night changes. In the northern Chilean Altiplano, summer precipitation can interrupt the dry-desert expectation. Climate labels here overlap: a maritime route can also be wet cold, and an alpine route can cross Mediterranean foothills.

## Weather characteristics

Aridity combines with elevation, solar exposure and potentially large day-to-night changes. In the northern Chilean Altiplano, summer precipitation can interrupt the dry-desert expectation.

## Main risks

Water scarcity, exposed wind, cold nights, strong sun, navigation mistakes and assuming all Chilean desert locations share one rainfall season.

## Recommended layering strategy

Wear lightweight sun coverage and control heat during effort. Carry wind protection and a night or stop reserve sized for elevation. Base water carrying and storm protection on the route and current forecast. This is a function-based starting point; no fixed temperature limit is inferred from a brand or fabric name. [NPS desert hike planning](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/arches-hike-planning.htm).

## Seasonal considerations

Do not copy a Northern Hemisphere desert calendar. Northern Chile’s summer rain regime and central Chile’s winter rain pattern differ.
