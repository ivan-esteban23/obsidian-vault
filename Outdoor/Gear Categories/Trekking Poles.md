# Trekking Poles

**Function:** Provide additional contact points and help manage repeated walking effort when used appropriately.

## Selection criteria

Compare length range, grip, locking system, packed dimensions and repairability. Select tips and baskets appropriate to the route and surface. [Trekking pole selection](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/trekking-poles-hiking-staffs.html).

## Use and field checks

Adjust and test locks before the trip. Secure poles when using hands for other tasks; they are aids rather than a solution for unsafe footing or extreme wind.

## Failure modes and tradeoffs

A slipping lock or damaged segment can remove the expected support. Pole choice does not make a hazardous crossing safe.
