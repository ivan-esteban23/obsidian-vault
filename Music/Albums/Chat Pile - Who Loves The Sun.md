# Chat Pile — Who Loves The Sun

![[attachments/Alternative 2026/20-chat-pile.jpg|320]]

**4 September 2026** · Album · Noise rock / industrial and sludge elements · The Flenser

Chat Pile's third full-length turns its attention to collective indifference, technological pressure and the isolation of Oklahoma City. The band records the album itself, with Ben Greenberg mixing. Its description retains noise rock as a foundation while noting an increased interest in hooks and older melodic influences.
