# Citizen — Halcyon Blues

![[attachments/Alternative 2026/09-citizen.jpg|320]]

**7 August 2026** · Album · Alternative rock / emo / indie-pop elements · Run For Cover Records

Citizen draws together strands from its changing catalogue on an eleven-track album recorded in Toledo. Mat Kerekes engineers, while the whole band writes and produces. The title track includes flute, with strings appearing elsewhere; the release notes connect the record to emo, alternative and indie-pop elements.
