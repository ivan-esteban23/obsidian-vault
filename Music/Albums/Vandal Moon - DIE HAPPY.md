# Vandal Moon — DIE HAPPY

![[attachments/Goth Rock 2026/14-vandal-moon.jpg|320]]

**13 February 2026** · Album · Darkwave / new wave · Gothdammit Records

A dystopian concept record about technology and obsession, Die Happy uses Vandal Moon's mixture of synthesizers, guitars and drum machines. Rusty Santos produced it and Maurizio Baggio mixed it, with Sister Nocturne contributing guest vocals. Its darkwave route is grounded in the band's stated early-eighties new wave and gothic references.

**First spin:** Heaven Without You.
