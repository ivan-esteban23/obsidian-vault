# Active Insulation

**Function:** Balance heat retention and moisture movement while working hard in cold conditions.

## Selection criteria

Look for a construction designed for exertion, then assess face-fabric air permeability, venting and abrasion zones. Active insulation is a role, not one fiber or branded fabric. [Layering functions and exertion](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/layering-basics.html).

## Use and field checks

Test at the expected walking pace. If it is comfortable at a cold trailhead but excessive after ten minutes uphill, it may belong in the pack or be replaced by a lighter layer.

## Failure modes and tradeoffs

Breathability can allow wind through; more protection can reduce heat escape during effort.
