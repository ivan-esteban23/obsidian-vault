# Waves Crashing — In The Blur

![[attachments/Alternative 2026/26-waves-crashing.jpg|320]]

**20 May 2026** · Album · Shoegaze / alternative rock / post-punk · Audiomanic Records

Waves Crashing describes an eight-track meeting of shoegaze atmosphere, post-punk pulse and melodic alternative rock. Joshua Calisti's voice, guitar and cello sit with Bryce Albright's drums and Zach Olson's bass. Feel The Glow opens this self-written and self-produced collection.

**First spin:** Feel The Glow.
