# Winter

**Southern Hemisphere default:** June–August. These are meteorological planning months, not guaranteed snow, rain or park-opening dates. Northern Hemisphere use requires shifting the calendar and checking the local climate.

## Environmental characteristics

Central Chile’s wet season and mountain snow contrast with cold exposed southern conditions. Access, guides and service availability can differ from the summer season. For Chile, distinguish [[Outdoor/Regional Guides/Central Chile Mediterranean Climate|Central Chile Mediterranean Climate]] from [[Outdoor/Regional Guides/Patagonia Region|Patagonia Region]] and the [[Outdoor/Regional Guides/Chilean Andes|Chilean Andes]]. [Chile national climate communication, 2016](https://mma.gob.cl/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/TCN-2016b1.pdf).

## Layering recommendations

Use a moisture-managed base, adjustable moving insulation, a protective shell and a substantial stop reserve. Assess the sleeping mat and bag together, and size footwear and gloves for the intended conditions. See [[Outdoor/Layering Systems/Moving vs Static Insulation|Moving vs Static Insulation]] for the transition between effort and rest.

## Common mistakes

Wearing all insulation uphill; treating an emergency bag temperature as comfort; assuming clothing replaces avalanche knowledge or that summer park access rules apply.
