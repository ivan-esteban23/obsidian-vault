# Darkswoon — Antivenom

![[attachments/Goth Rock 2026/16-darkswoon.jpg|320]]

**3 April 2026** · Album · Darkwave / post-punk / shoegaze · Viasonde

Darkswoon's fourth album places Jana Cushman's voice and guitar against hardware electronics, melodic bass and programmed rhythms. The release notes connect darkwave and post-punk with a wider shoegaze reach, while the lyrics address loss, anxiety and inequality. The title track provides a focused entrance into that combination of mechanical texture and personal writing.

**First spin:** Antivenom.
