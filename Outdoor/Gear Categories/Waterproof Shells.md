# Waterproof Shells

**Function:** Provide the outer barrier against sustained rain or wet snow when the construction and closures suit the task.

## Selection criteria

Check seam sealing, hood adjustment, cuffs, ventilation, hem coverage, pocket access and abrasion under straps. Two-, two-and-a-half- and three-layer labels describe construction rather than how many garments to wear. [Rainwear selection](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/rainwear.html).

## Use and field checks

Try it over the planned insulation, then raise your arms and tighten a hipbelt. Clean and maintain it according to the specific label. Wet-out of the face fabric is different from a torn membrane or leaking seam.

## Failure modes and tradeoffs

A waterproof shell can still trap sweat. Laboratory breathability numbers from different methods are not directly interchangeable.
