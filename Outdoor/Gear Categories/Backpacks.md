# Backpacks

**Function:** Carry the complete load comfortably while keeping essential layers accessible.

## Selection criteria

Select torso length, hipbelt fit and suspension for the actual carried load. Capacity follows food, shelter and sleep volume; do not buy a large pack merely because the trip lasts several days. [Backpack selection](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/backpack.html).

## Use and field checks

Test loaded with the bulkiest likely kit, then reach shell, gloves and water without a complete unpack. Keep rain-sensitive insulation inside an independent waterproof barrier.

## Failure modes and tradeoffs

A durable face fabric does not make seams, openings and zippers watertight; a pack cover is not a full immersion barrier.
