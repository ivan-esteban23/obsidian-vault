# Doutzen Kroes – runway locations

A location check for the runway collection. These are **places where a show occurred**, not the country where the label was founded. Research checked 11 November 2026. Not finding a result does not establish that an appearance never happened.

[[Doutzen Kroes - Italian and French Runway References|Open the runway gallery]] – [[Doutzen Kroes - Runway Explorer.canvas|Open the runway Canvas]]

## Verified appearances

- **New York City, United States** – [[Oscar de la Renta - Doutzen Kroes|Oscar de la Renta]], Fall/Winter 2005–2006, and [[Proenza Schouler - Doutzen Kroes|Proenza Schouler]], Fall/Winter 2020–2021. [Oscar de la Renta show record](https://tectova.com/shows/oscar-de-la-renta/fw2005-rtw) – [Vogue Netherlands: runway retrospective](https://www.vogue.nl/fashion/models/doutzen-kroes-catwalkmomenten/)
- **Los Angeles, United States** – Victoria's Secret Fashion Show, 18 October 2026. This is a lingerie show, included here as a location match. [Victoria's Secret: show and cast](https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/vs/fashion-show/shop-the-show)
- **London, England** – [[Giles Deacon - Doutzen Kroes|Giles]], Fall/Winter 2012–2013, at London Fashion Week. [Runway cast and event](https://fashiondb.com/check-in/giles-fw-12)
- **Paris, France** – [[Jacquemus - Doutzen Kroes|Jacquemus]], Spring/Summer 2025. [Vogue Netherlands: show, model and location](https://www.vogue.nl/fashion/doutzen-kroes-imaan-hammam-jacquemus-show-lente-zomer-2025/)
- **Milan, Italy** – [[Fendi - Doutzen Kroes|Fendi]], Fall/Winter 2015–2016. [Vogue: Kroes at Fendi in Milan](https://www.vogue.com/article/doutzen-kroes-karlie-kloss-backstage-milan)

## Not yet verified

Searches did not identify a reliable source establishing a runway appearance by Kroes in **Canada, San Francisco, Washington, Wales, Ireland, Scotland, Portugal, Spain, South Africa, Sweden, Norway, the Netherlands, Finland, Russia, Latvia, the Czech Republic, Poland, China, Japan, South Korea, Thailand, Turkey, Australia or New Zealand**. Washington was checked as a place name without assuming whether it means the state or Washington, D.C. The checks do not rule out appearances that are less well documented.

A reported **Desigual booking in Barcelona in January 2014** was announced in advance, but contemporary reporting says Irina Shayk replaced Kroes, so it is not counted as a verified appearance. [Europa Press report](https://www.europapress.tv/reportajes/213305/1/supermodelo-rusa-irina-shayk-sido-cara-mas-visible-nueva-linea-why-desigual)

## Designer origin is not show location

[[Ulyana Sergeenko - Doutzen Kroes|Ulyana Sergeenko]] is a Russian designer, and Kroes walked her Spring 2017 couture show **in Paris**. This is a match for a Russian designer, not evidence of a runway show in Russia. [Vogue show review](https://www.vogue.com/fashion-shows/spring-2017-couture/ulyana-sergeenko) – [Designer and location context](https://fashionnewsdelivery.wordpress.com/2017/01/30/georgian-models-shine-at-paris-fashion-week/)
