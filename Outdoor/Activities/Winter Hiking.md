# Winter Hiking

## Activity demands

Winter walking can move from maintained paths to exposed snow terrain. Reduced daylight and the consequences of a stop make reserves more important.

## Layering strategy

Keep moving insulation adjustable and carry dry stop clothing, spare hand protection and sufficient leg coverage. Check that closures work without removing all hand protection. [Layering functions and exertion](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/layering-basics.html).

## Gear decisions

Select traction and footwear for the actual terrain; microspikes are not a universal substitute for mountaineering equipment or skills. Check access and forecasts before departure.
