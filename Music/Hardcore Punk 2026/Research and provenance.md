# Research and provenance

**Verified:** 15 November 2026. **Scope:** the ten approved releases in [[Hardcore Punk 2026]], [[Music/Genres/Hardcore Punk|the hub]] and [[Music/Hardcore Punk 2026/Hardcore Punk Explorer.canvas|the explorer]].

## Method and caveats

Official artist/label Bandcamp pages supply release dates, track lists, format statements, credits, tags and artist context. The HTML `data-tralbum` current release ID supplies each numeric player ID; `album_release_date` agrees with the displayed release credits. The generic “Digital Album” purchase heading is not used as LP/EP evidence. Undocumented classifications are left unspecified. No supplied release date or track count required correction.

M.U.G.’s September 1 date is the digital release credit, not vinyl shipping. STAGNATE is Indonesian; the London profile belongs to Inflammable Material. The filenames replace the slash in S/T EP with a hyphen. All release notes retain the vault’s `type/album` convention, including EPs. Routes are editorial groupings, and no new influence edges or changes to existing record classifications are made.

## Listening and verification limits

Local artwork works offline; Bandcamp streaming requires internet and may depend on the player’s service or client settings. Summaries are original accounts of the cited metadata, not listening reviews. No promotional paragraphs or song lyrics are reproduced. Unnamed labels, cover artists and undocumented LP/EP status remain unverified as listed above.

## Collection validation

Checks on 15 November 2026 confirmed exactly ten distinct releases across the four approved routes, valid YAML, resolving internal file/heading links (including M.U.G.’s dotted filename), unique Canvas IDs, valid file references and edge endpoints, non-overlapping cards, and preservation of every original genre-map node and all five edges. All ten unmodified JPEG covers opened successfully at 1200 pixels wide. All ten official player URLs returned HTTP 200 with matching artists and release titles.

In Obsidian 1.15.1, the magazine and both Canvases were inspected in light and dark themes. Every explorer record card fit its text; the expanded map navigation was adjusted in scoped CSS to fit its original heading card. The first embedded player loaded its matching track list in the app. Audio playback was not tested, and network availability remains necessary for streaming.
