# Moisture and Sweat Management

The moisture problem has two sources: weather entering from outside and perspiration generated inside. Waterproof clothing addresses only part of the system.

## How to decide

Vent early, keep effort sustainable and separate drying priorities. Base layers and socks may need rotation; the sleep reserve deserves a more reliable dry boundary. Fabric breathability cannot guarantee that the whole outfit stays dry at every workload. [Layering functions and exertion](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/layering-basics.html).

## Put it into practice

At camp, keep wet rainwear away from sleeping insulation and use safe ventilation rather than trying to dry everything with a stove. Treat limited drying opportunities as a multi-day constraint, not an inconvenience to solve after the first soaked night.

## Failure to avoid

Common mistake: adding warmer layers to compensate for every damp sensation. First assess whether sweat, rain entry or a colder rest has changed the problem.
