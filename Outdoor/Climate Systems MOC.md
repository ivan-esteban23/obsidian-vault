# Climate Systems MOC

Choose the most consequential conditions along the route. The eight notes describe overlapping planning contexts rather than mutually exclusive climate classifications. A brand shortlist follows the functional need; it does not replace the route forecast.

## Climate notes

- [[Outdoor/Climates/Wet Cold|Wet Cold]] — Wear a light base and adjustable moving insulation.
- [[Outdoor/Climates/Wet Temperate|Wet Temperate]] — Use a lightweight base and little worn insulation during effort.
- [[Outdoor/Climates/Dry Cold|Dry Cold]] — Keep the moving outfit lean enough to avoid sweating heavily.
- [[Outdoor/Climates/Alpine|Alpine]] — Choose a moisture-managed moving outfit, wind or storm protection that permits reach, and separate stop insulation.
- [[Outdoor/Climates/Maritime|Maritime]] — Prioritize reliable closures and rain coverage, then regulate heat with ventilation.
- [[Outdoor/Climates/Sub-Arctic|Sub-Arctic]] — Combine maintainable trekking layers with wind and rain reserves.
- [[Outdoor/Climates/Mediterranean|Mediterranean]] — In summer, prioritize breathable sun coverage and ventilation.
- [[Outdoor/Climates/High Desert|High Desert]] — Wear lightweight sun coverage and control heat during effort.
