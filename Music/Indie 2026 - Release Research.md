# Indie — 2026 Release Research

Checked **14 November 2026** for [[Small Signals - Indie 2026|Small Signals]]. Thirty releases by thirty artists were checked directly against their artist or label pages. All selected digital editions are available, have at least three tracks and fall within the 2026 release window.

## Selection and sources

The Indie and Alternative magazines contain sixty distinct releases in total. A record appears in one feature list, while its supported musical connections remain available across genre hubs. Established acts and smaller discoveries are both included; the issue makes no popularity ranking.

Dates, track lists, artwork and players follow artist or label release pages. Descriptions paraphrase the documented release information and credits; starting tracks are editorial entry points. Supporting album editorials and reviews are linked where they establish additional context. Independent-label status and generic catalogue tags alone do not establish a musical genre.

Covers are downloaded from the cited release pages; the artists and original rights holders retain ownership. A label account’s location is not assigned to its artists. “Artist Bandcamp edition” describes the cited edition without asserting that no label is involved.

## Edition notes

- **Paper Citizen:** navigation shortens the storefront title *Embers LP* to *Embers*. The full title remains in the album note.
- **Tokyo Tea Room / Girlhood:** current release data confirms that both cited digital editions are released; older search snapshots still describe preorders.
- **Locket Protector / The Simple Present:** their pages explicitly describe an EP and mini-album, respectively; both are included alongside full-length albums.
- **Exclusions:** single-track listings, pre-2026 originals and remaster/reissue-only editions were excluded during research. Physical shipping dates are not substituted for digital release dates.
