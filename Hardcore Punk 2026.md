# Hardcore Punk

*2026 · Listening issue*

**Ten releases. Ten artists. Four listening routes.**

Hardcore can move through punk economy, melodic urgency, metallic weight and beatdown. This issue follows ten modern releases across that broad field, with short sets beside a full LP and each record appearing once. The routes are invitations to listen; the source notes retain the distinctions between them.
