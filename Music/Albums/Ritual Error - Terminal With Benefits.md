# Ritual Error — Terminal With Benefits

![[attachments/Alternative 2026/22-ritual-error.jpg|320]]

**25 September 2026** · Album · Noise rock / post-hardcore / post-punk · Totality

Ritual Error reconvenes with Wayne Adams at Bear Bites Horse for a second album described as more angular than its debut. The release notes emphasize abrasive bass, shrill guitar and precise drumming, with surreal narratives and J. G. Ballard references. Tapeworm follows its brief opening track.

**First spin:** Tapeworm.
