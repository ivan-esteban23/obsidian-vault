# Emma Cook — Of the Morning

![[attachments/Indie 2026/16-emma-cook.jpg|320]]

**24 February 2026** · Album · Indie folk / folk pop · Artist Bandcamp edition

Emma Cook presents Of the Morning as folk music for the ordinary pressures and joys of contemporary life. The credits bring acoustic guitar, piano, brass and a changing rhythm section around her voice. Thirteen Moons opens a record intended for gardens, drives and moments of slowing down.

**First spin:** Thirteen Moons.
