# Dark Ambient

A genre hub for exploring dark ambient connections in this collection.

## Album-level elements

- [[Music/Albums/Worm - Necropalace|Worm — Necropalace]] — [Metallized.it](https://www.metallized.it/recensione.php?id=21394) describes the opening track as a dark ambient intro.

- [[Music/Black Metal 2026 Demo/Albums/Panopticon - Det hjemsøkte hjertet|Panopticon — Det hjemsøkte hjertet]] — a [review](https://veilofsound.com/2026/05/25/Panopticon-Det_Hjems%C3%B8kte_Hjertet.html) identifies dark ambient passages.
- [[Music/Albums/Diabolus, Mecum Semperterne! - Diabolus, Mecum Semperterne!|Diabolus, Mecum Semperterne! — Diabolus, Mecum Semperterne!]] — [Metalitalia](https://metalitalia.com/album/diabolus-mecum-semperterne-diabolus-mecum-semperterne/) describes the closing “Postludium” as a dark ambient coda.
- [[Music/Albums/Antrisch - Expedition III - Renitenzpfad|Antrisch — Expedition III: Renitenzpfad]] — an [album review](https://occultblackmetalzine.blogspot.com/2026/02/antrischexpedition-iii-renitenzpfadaop.html) identifies dark ambient elements. The [label](https://artofpropaganda.bandcamp.com/album/expedition-iii-renitenzpfad) also describes this as an element of the project's sound.

These are dark ambient elements within black metal records, not a claim that the records are primarily dark ambient. The distinction between album evidence and project-level descriptions is retained in each note.
