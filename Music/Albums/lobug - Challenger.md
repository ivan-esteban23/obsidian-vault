# lobug — Challenger

**14 August 2026** · Album · Grunge / alternative rock / shoegaze · Artist Bandcamp edition

Ashton Zautke makes lobug's second album explicitly autobiographical, writing about feeling left behind and losing a childhood dog. Zautke writes, performs, produces and mixes the nine songs. Motorbike offers an entry into a release that labels its bedroom-recorded guitar world with grunge and shoegaze tags.
