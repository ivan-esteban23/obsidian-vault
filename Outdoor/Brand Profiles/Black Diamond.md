# Black Diamond

**Country:** United States · **Origin:** United States · **Region:** American · **Cost:** Premium. Country identifies the brand base used here, not where every product is manufactured.

## Brand philosophy

Climbing and alpinism supply the design reference; clothing and accessories should be evaluated against those tasks. [Black Diamond — design / technical evidence](https://eu.blackdiamondequipment.com/pages/product-faq).

## Strengths

A strong specialist to compare for poles and mountain gloves. Its clothing documentation distinguishes different BD.dry constructions.

## Weaknesses and tradeoffs

Expertise in climbing hardware does not automatically make every garment a leading trekking option. Avoid treating all BD.dry products as a single specification. These are editorial selection tradeoffs, not measured defect rates.
