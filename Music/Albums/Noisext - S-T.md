# Noisext — S/T

![[attachments/Alternative 2026/17-noisext.jpg|320]]

**12 June 2026** · Album · Noise rock · Flamingo Records / Taxi Driver Records

Noisext returns to songs written before its original split, recording them anew in late 2025 for this release. The band describes a nine-track noise-rock album rather than a remaster of an older LP. Its long-delayed material reaches a finished studio form through Flamingo and Taxi Driver Records.
