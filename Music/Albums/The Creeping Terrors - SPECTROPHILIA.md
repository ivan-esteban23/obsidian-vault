# The Creeping Terrors — SPECTROPHILIA

![[attachments/Goth Rock 2026/13-the-creeping-terrors.jpg|320]]

**31 October 2026** · Album · Deathrock / horror punk / post-punk · Bat-Cave Productions / Nox Production

Released for Halloween, Spectrophilia brings phantom lovers, witches and ghostly wrestlers into a feminist post-punk setting. The Creeping Terrors describes its broader approach as a meeting of post-punk riot grrrl and horror deathrock. Twelve songs make this an extended seasonal excursion rather than a single Halloween novelty.
