*Goth Rock & the Wider Scene · 2026*

**Thirty releases. Thirty artists. Four ways into the night.**

Gothic rock is the starting point for this issue, with deathrock, darkwave and goth post-punk given listening routes of their own. Pick a section, open a cover and follow a song. The numbers identify entries; this is a listening collection, not a ranking.
