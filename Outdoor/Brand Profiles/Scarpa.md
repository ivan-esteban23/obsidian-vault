# Scarpa

**Country:** Italy · **Origin:** Italy · **Region:** European · **Cost:** Premium. Country identifies the brand base used here, not where every product is manufactured.

## Brand philosophy

Footwear function and continued service through care and resoling are central to its mountain-equipment role. [Scarpa — design / technical evidence](https://scarpa.com/pages/long-life-design-resole-project).

## Strengths

A serious footwear comparison for trekking and mountain travel. The official resoling program explains the need to repair before wear becomes too severe.

## Weaknesses and tradeoffs

Not every shoe has the same last, outsole or repair eligibility. A stiff mountain boot can be an inefficient choice for easy warm-weather trails. These are editorial selection tradeoffs, not measured defect rates.
