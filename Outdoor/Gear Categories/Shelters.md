# Shelters

**Function:** Provide weather protection, a workable living space and an overnight drying boundary.

## Selection criteria

Compare architecture, pole support, guy points, fly coverage, ventilation and pitching method. A season label is a useful description, not a universal wind-speed certificate. [Backpacking shelter selection](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/backpacking-tent.html).

## Use and field checks

Practice with the complete pole and stake set. In exposed Patagonia, use permitted sites and all manufacturer-required supports; site selection can matter as much as specification.

## Failure modes and tradeoffs

Low weight alone does not establish high-wind suitability. Interior condensation is not automatically fabric leakage.
