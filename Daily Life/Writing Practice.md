# Writing Practice

Pick a small subject you care about: an album, a book, an idea, or something that happened today. Write for ten minutes without editing, then take five minutes to revise.

## Revision questions

1. What is the one thing I want the reader to understand?
2. Does the opening give them a reason to keep reading?
3. Can I replace a vague sentence with a specific detail or example?
4. Does each paragraph add something useful? Read the piece aloud for rhythm.
5. Compare the first and revised versions. What did I learn for next time?

## Get feedback in Obsidian

In an English draft, run **Write Good: Toggle checks for current note** from the command palette. Review the highlighted suggestions one at a time. They are prompts, not rules: passive voice, adverbs, and repetition can be useful when they serve your meaning. Run the command again to turn checks off for that note. The existing Better Word Count and Reading Time plugins can help you understand the length of a draft.

## Practice log

Add the date, what you wrote, and one thing you noticed about your revision.
