# Headwear

**Function:** Manage sun, wind and heat loss without obstructing vision or the shell hood.

## Selection criteria

Choose a sun hat or cap for hot exposed travel and a warmer option for cold stops. Test how it works with the rain hood and any route-required helmet. [Layering functions and exertion](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/layering-basics.html).

## Use and field checks

Use secure but appropriate retention in wind. Store a dry warmer hat with the stop clothing rather than exposing every head layer to the same rain.

## Failure modes and tradeoffs

A loose brim can catch wind or interfere with the hood; a thick hat can cause overheating on ascent.
