# Dark Ambient — 2026 Album Research

Checked all 40 entries in [[Where the Darkness Gathers - Black Metal 2026|the black metal magazine]] against the artist or label release page linked below, and searched for album-specific dark ambient descriptions. Checked 11 November 2026. “Not verified” means the checked evidence did not establish the connection; it does not mean the album has no ambient passages or that no other source exists. Bandcamp recommendation and listener-comment text is not evidence about the featured album.

## Findings

- **Album-level element:** Worm — [Metallized.it](https://www.metallized.it/recensione.php?id=21394) describes the opening track “Gates to the Shadow Zone” as a dark ambient intro.

- **Album-level element:** Diabolus, Mecum Semperterne! — [Metalitalia](https://metalitalia.com/album/diabolus-mecum-semperterne-diabolus-mecum-semperterne/) describes “Postludium” as a dark ambient coda.
- **Album-level element:** Panopticon — [Veil of Sound](https://veilofsound.com/2026/05/25/Panopticon-Det_Hjems%C3%B8kte_Hjertet.html) describes dark ambient passages.
- **Album-level element:** Antrisch — [OccultBlackMetalZine](https://occultblackmetalzine.blogspot.com/2026/02/antrischexpedition-iii-renitenzpfadaop.html) identifies dark ambient elements on the album; the label separately describes a project-level influence.
- **Collaboration, not sufficient for this filter:** Flykt — [No Clean Singing](https://www.nocleansinging.com/2026/07/06/obscurities-volume-xxix-summer-solstice/) identifies Apocryphos as a dark ambient project contributing atmosphere, but does not specifically classify the album or passages as dark ambient. The reviewer discloses a friendship with the contributor.
- **Not verified:** JUNON — [Metalitalia](https://metalitalia.com/album/junon-the-golden-citadel-of-the-astral-sphere/) refers to a dark ambient track on the earlier demo and says that track is absent from this album.
