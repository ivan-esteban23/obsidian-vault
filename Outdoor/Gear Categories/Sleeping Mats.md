# Sleeping Mats

**Function:** Reduce conductive heat loss to the ground and provide a stable sleeping surface.

## Selection criteria

Compare standardized R-value, size, construction and repair options. Think of the mat and sleeping bag as one thermal system; more thickness does not necessarily mean more insulation. [Sleeping mats and R-value](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/sleeping-pads.html).

## Use and field checks

Test inflation and comfort before departure. Carry the compatible repair method and protect the mat from sharp ground.

## Failure modes and tradeoffs

Different testing bases can make older ratings difficult to compare. A puncture matters more when the mat is the only ground-insulation reserve.
