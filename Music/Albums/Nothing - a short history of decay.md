# Nothing — a short history of decay

![[attachments/Alternative 2026/25-nothing.jpg|320]]

**27 February 2026** · Album · Heavy shoegaze / grunge elements · Run For Cover Records

Domenic Palermo brings Nothing to Run For Cover with a fifth album that widens the band's range of volume and intimacy. The expanded line-up includes members associated with Cloakroom and Best Coast. Cannibal World follows the opener and offers an entrance into this heavy shoegaze setting.

**First spin:** cannibal world.
