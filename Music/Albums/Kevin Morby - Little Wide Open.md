# Kevin Morby — Little Wide Open

![[attachments/Indie 2026/21-kevin-morby.jpg|320]]

**15 May 2026** · Album · Songwriting / roots-oriented arrangements · Dead Oceans

Kevin Morby's eighth studio album returns to the people and landscapes of the Midwest. Aaron Dessner produces, with sessions at Long Pond and contributions from several guest musicians. Morby places it after Sundowner and This Is a Photograph in a continuing account of returning home.

**First spin:** Javelin.
