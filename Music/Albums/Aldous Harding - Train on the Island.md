# Aldous Harding — Train on the Island

![[attachments/Indie 2026/20-aldous-harding.jpg|320]]

**8 May 2026** · Album · Folk / chamber arrangements · 4AD

Aldous Harding reunites with John Parish for a fifth album recorded at Rockfield in Wales. Pedal steel, harp, synthesizer and H. Hawkline's multiple instrumental roles broaden the arrangements. Coats closes the ten-track sequence and offers a starting point for this latest collaboration.
