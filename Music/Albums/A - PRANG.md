# A — PRANG

![[attachments/Alternative 2026/07-a.jpg|320]]

**22 May 2026** · Album · Alternative rock / pop-punk · Cooking Vinyl

A's first studio album in more than twenty years reunites its reflective writing with riff-led songs and open-hearted choruses. Jason Perry produces, and Hello Sunshine introduces the ten-track set. The release notes connect its themes to gratitude, mental health, resilience and long friendship.
