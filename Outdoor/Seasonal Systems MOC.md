# Seasonal Systems MOC

Use Southern Hemisphere meteorological seasons for Chile. Calendar months provide an organizing convention; snow cover, daylight, weather systems and park access must be checked for the actual route.

## Seasonal routes

- [[Outdoor/Seasons/Spring|Spring]] — September–November.
- [[Outdoor/Seasons/Summer|Summer]] — December–February.
- [[Outdoor/Seasons/Autumn|Autumn]] — March–May.
- [[Outdoor/Seasons/Winter|Winter]] — June–August.

## Place matters

[[Outdoor/Regional Guides/Central Chile Mediterranean Climate|Central Chile Mediterranean Climate]] · [[Outdoor/Regional Guides/Chilean Andes|Chilean Andes]] · [[Outdoor/Regional Guides/Patagonia Region|Patagonia Region]] · [[Outdoor/Regional Guides/Torres del Paine|Torres del Paine]].

Summer in a central valley and summer on an exposed Patagonian pass require different reserves. Northern Chile’s Altiplano rainfall pattern should not be copied from central Chile.
