# Wet Temperate

**Planning context:** Rain is frequent or persistent without sustained extreme cold. Humidity and vegetation can keep both clothing and footwear damp even between showers. Climate labels here overlap: a maritime route can also be wet cold, and an alpine route can cross Mediterranean foothills.

## Weather characteristics

Rain is frequent or persistent without sustained extreme cold. Humidity and vegetation can keep both clothing and footwear damp even between showers.

## Main risks

Sweat accumulation under rainwear, wet feet, rubbing under pack straps, and assuming mild temperatures make dry camp clothing unnecessary.

## Recommended layering strategy

Use a lightweight base and little worn insulation during effort. Carry an independently adjustable waterproof shell and choose legwear that does not depend on staying dry to remain manageable. This is a function-based starting point; no fixed temperature limit is inferred from a brand or fabric name. [Rainwear selection](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/rainwear.html).

## Seasonal considerations

Warm summer rain increases the ventilation problem; autumn adds shorter drying windows and colder stops.
