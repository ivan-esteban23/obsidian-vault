# Devold

**Country:** Norway · **Origin:** Norway · **Region:** Scandinavian · **Cost:** Premium. Country identifies the brand base used here, not where every product is manufactured.

## Brand philosophy

Wool next-to-skin systems are the core specialty, with different garment weights serving different effort levels. [Devold — Expedition Merino 235 construction](https://www.devold.com/en-de/product/expedition-merino-235-shirt-man-go155224a/?color=tarmac).

## Strengths

Useful for repeated days when odor management and a comfortable base layer matter. Expedition-weight garments belong to a different use case from lightweight trekking tops.

## Weaknesses and tradeoffs

Wool can retain substantial moisture and dry slowly; fine fabrics need care under straps. A warm wool top is neither a wind barrier nor a rain layer. These are editorial selection tradeoffs, not measured defect rates.
