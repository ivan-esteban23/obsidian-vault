# Sources and Review Log

**Research date:** 15 November 2026. This is a sourced reference collection with original editorial summaries, not a record of hands-on equipment tests.

## Source hierarchy

Official brand documentation establishes named technologies, product constructions, published policies and stated practices. Park authorities and Chilean government publications establish local planning context. REI educational guides explain equipment-selection functions; they are retailer-authored educational sources, not independent comparative tests. Where no independent comparison was established, precise brand-score differences remain low confidence.

## Corrections and important distinctions

- G-1000 wax supplies water resistance, not full waterproofness. H₂No is a performance standard, not one fabric or membrane.
- Arc’teryx is Canadian; it is a supplemental comparator outside the eight US brands.
- Salewa’s German origin and Italian base are recorded separately. British European brands are not described as geographically Alpine.
- Ortovox’s 2025 report says RWS replaced its earlier Wool Promise within the supply chain.
- Vaude’s current disclosures describe the 2026 independent Green Shape certification context; Fair Wear membership is not represented as product or factory certification.
- Haglöfs published a September 2026 notice about changes to sustainability information. Product evidence takes precedence over old hangtags.
- Older source years, including La Sportiva’s 2023 report and Vaude’s 2023 membrane explanation, are retained in their titles or documents. They are not treated as new audits.

## What remains unverified

Current Chilean prices, dealer stock, delivery, local repair eligibility and service turnaround have not been established. No personal measurements, equipment inventory or specific itinerary was supplied. Ratings do not measure defect rates, comparable lifecycle footprints or a universal best brand. No exact temperature or wind limit is assigned to a brand. Field systems must be adapted to route, effort and forecast.

The Torres del Paine bulletin is a dated planning source. Recheck official restrictions, reservations, trail status and cooking arrangements before travel; this library does not promise current route access.

## Review and maintenance

Review brand recommendations annually and whenever products, policies or certification schemes change. Refresh park and route information before each trip. Update the relevant frontmatter, rationale, static matrices and verification date together.

## Source access notes

The link audit found moved or retired paths. Norrøna’s older ETI PDF was replaced with its current responsibility page plus its repair policy; Salewa’s old report link was replaced with its current responsibility page. The Devold regional-domain guide was replaced with an official current product page. The park bulletin is linked through its official publication page, which supplies the current Spanish and English downloads. Ortovox’s report uses the current www host rather than the cms host that redirected to the homepage.

Several retail sites reject or time out automated CLI requests; their cited content was inspected through available web access instead. A CLI HTTP status alone was not treated as content verification, and an accessible homepage redirect was not accepted as a report. No access restriction or TLS validation was disabled.

## Validation record

Structural checks cover all 100 notes, their required properties, rating ranges, internal targets, table syntax and MOC reachability. All 50 DQL queries executed successfully against Dataview 0.5.68 in Obsidian 1.15.1. The four MOCs, representative profiles, three recommendation matrices, Torres del Paine and a field-system note were inspected in both light and dark themes. No hands-on gear tests were performed.
