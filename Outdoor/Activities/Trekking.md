# Trekking

## Activity demands

Walking routes where sustained movement, pack contact and changing weather dominate clothing decisions. Day routes and hut-supported itineraries still need a reserve for an unplanned stop.

## Layering strategy

Use a breathable base, optional fleece, an adjustable wind/rain barrier and a protected stop layer. Test legwear under a hipbelt. [Layering functions and exertion](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/layering-basics.html).

## Gear decisions

Choose footwear by fit and terrain before brand, and pack navigation and rain protection where they can be reached without unpacking everything.

## Common mistakes

Overdressing at the start; inaccessible rainwear; testing new footwear on the main trip.
