# All-Round Outdoor Systems

An all-round system covers the trip’s functions with compatible components. No brand in this collection is established as the best source for every category or climate.

## Patterns and counterexamples

| Pattern | Evidence-informed interpretation | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clothing-first | Patagonia is a strong modular starting point; Norrøna and Arc’teryx are premium technical-apparel alternatives. | Add footwear and overnight equipment independently. |
| Insulation-first | Rab, Mountain Equipment and Mountain Hardwear deserve attention when warmth and sleep equipment dominate. | Verify fit, moisture protection and the mat/bag pairing. |
| General trekking equipment | Vaude, REI Co-op, Fjällräven and Bergans offer useful breadth. | Catalogue breadth does not certify every component for exposed weather. |
| Specialists within a mixed system | Devold or Ortovox for a wool role; Scarpa or La Sportiva for appropriate footwear; Outdoor Research for accessories. | Select by the specific role and fit rather than trying to equalize brand counts. |

## Practical implication

For multi-day Chilean trekking, prioritize a balanced mixed-brand system: compatible moving layers, controlled storm protection, protected stop insulation, fitted footwear and an exposure-appropriate overnight kit. Repair existing equipment and replace a functional gap before optimizing a logo or a one-point rating difference.
