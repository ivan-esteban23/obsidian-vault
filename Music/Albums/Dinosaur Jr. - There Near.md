# Dinosaur Jr. — There Near

![[attachments/Alternative 2026/13-dinosaur-jr.jpg|320]]

**28 August 2026** · Album · Alternative rock / noise rock / grunge · Jagjaguwar

Dinosaur Jr. records another set with its core trio at Amherst's Bisquiteen Studio, with Ken Mauri adding piano and organ. The release notes emphasize short, intense sessions and the interaction of J Mascis, Lou Barlow and Murph. Several Got Away introduces eleven new songs.

**First spin:** Several Got Away.
