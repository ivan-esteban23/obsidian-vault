# Fleece Midlayers

**Function:** Provide adjustable insulation with relatively simple care and useful ventilation.

## Selection criteria

Compare fabric weight, air permeability, zip length and bulk under the shell. A highly open fleece needs a wind barrier sooner; a thick fleece may be excessive while climbing with a pack. [Layering functions and exertion](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/layering-basics.html).

## Use and field checks

Use the zipper and pace to manage heat before sweat saturates the base. At a windy stop, add protection over the fleece rather than expecting it to block the wind.

## Failure modes and tradeoffs

Fleece can dry more readily than some alternatives but still becomes wet and adds pack volume.
