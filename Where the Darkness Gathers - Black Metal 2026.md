## 01 Misotheist — De Pinte

[[Music/Black Metal 2026 Demo/Albums/Misotheist - De Pinte|Album note and graph connections]]

![[attachments/Black Metal 2026/01-misotheist.jpg|600]]

**20 February 2026** · Norway · Dissonant / atmospheric black metal · Terratur Possessions

<iframe title="Misotheist — De Pinte - Bandcamp player" style="border: 0; width: 100%; max-width: 600px; height: 380px;" src="https://bandcamp.com/EmbeddedPlayer/album=4116769039/size=large/bgcol=333333/linkcol=e99708/artwork=small/transparent=true/" loading="lazy"></iframe>

### The band
Misotheist is a Norwegian black metal project that keeps its members' identities in the background. The Trondheim label Terratur Possessions presents *De Pinte* as the band's fourth album.

### The record
Four tracks spread across roughly 43 minutes, with aggression and melancholy leading into a 21-minute title track. This is a good entry point if you want an intense record that gives its ideas room to unfold.

> [!tip] First spin
> **“Blinded and Revealed”** for an introduction; **“De Pinte”** when you are ready for the long form. [Listen and read the label's notes](https://terraturpossessions.bandcamp.com/album/de-pinte).

### The band
Panopticon is Austin Lunn's project, closely associated with atmospheric black metal and reflections on landscape. The artist's notes credit Lunn with songwriting and most instruments, and Charlie Anderson with orchestral arrangements and strings.

The title translates as *The Haunted Heart*. Its narrative follows an elderly hermit's final week, bringing memories of childhood into contact with a changing Minnesota wilderness. Guest vocalists and long arrangements give the story different voices.

## More underground YouTube channels

Alongside Black Metal Promotion, these channels offer more paths into underground releases. Promotion channels cover multiple artists; label channels generally focus on their own catalog. Check each video's description for the band, label and release date—an upload date is not necessarily the release date.

- **[Atmospheric Black Metal Albums](https://www.youtube.com/@atmosphericblackmetalalbum1807)** — atmospheric black metal and related styles, including full-album streams. A good first stop for long, immersive records. [Channel listing](https://socialblade.com/youtube/handle/atmosphericblackmetalalbum1807).
- **[Transmissions from the Dark](https://www.youtube.com/transmissionsfromthedark)** — underground black metal premieres and full albums. The channel's [official link page](https://linktr.ee/transmissionsftd) also points to separate channels for dungeon synth and other metal; the Dutch label Void Wanderer highlighted its full stream of Hagetisse's 2026 album *To Wither Beneath Thy Radiance*. [Label announcement](https://voidwanderer.com/hagetisse-to-wither-beneath-thy-radiance-2/).
- **[Only Black Metal](https://www.youtube.com/@onlyblackmetal)** — an underground-focused black metal channel with albums and compilations. [Channel operator's profile](https://bandcamp.com/darkenedspirit).
- **[Naturmacht Productions](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UChn3qiZ9HgJ-QsdXE4sKWPA)** — the Finnish label's channel, useful for atmospheric black metal and related sounds. It has featured Nordicwinter, already in this issue. [Label information](https://naturmacht.com/about-naturmacht-productions/).
- **[Ván Records](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCeLQW6nVKuC8Qyo_TxX5siQ)** — German label channel featuring black metal, full albums, music videos and live recordings; Fyrnask is one connection to this issue. [Channel overview](https://socialdiscovery.app/en/youtube/channel/vanrecords).
- **[Debemur Morti Productions](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UChSPFhlKhvjpvBhO1gyDhVQ)** — French label channel with full albums and videos from its extreme-metal catalog. Its artists in this issue include Miserere Luminis, Pure Wrath and Aversio Humanitatis. [Channel overview](https://socialdiscovery.app/en/youtube/channel/debemur-morti-productions).
