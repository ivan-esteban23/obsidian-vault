# Paper Citizen — Embers LP

![[attachments/Indie 2026/08-paper-citizen.jpg|320]]

**7 August 2026** · Album · Indie rock / pop rock · Artist Bandcamp edition

Claire Gohst introduces Paper Citizen's debut through questions of grief, migration, belonging and chosen family. The twelve songs span several years, with Gohst and Sutter Hellwarth sharing production. The title refers to what survives a fire: memory, warmth and the chance to start again.

**First spin:** Heart On Fire.
