# Trekking Legwear

**Function:** Provide mobility, abrasion management and appropriate weather coverage below the waist.

## Selection criteria

Choose fabric and venting for the route. Durable woven trousers can be useful in brush, while lighter options may be preferable on hot open paths. Carry rain trousers when the exposure warrants them. [Fjällräven G-1000 care and repair](https://www.fjallraven.com/uk/en-gb/about-fjallraven/sustainability/care-repair/g-1000/).

## Use and field checks

Squat and step high with the hipbelt fastened. Check that wet cuffs do not drag and that vents remain useful beneath the pack.

## Failure modes and tradeoffs

Waxed fabric offers adjustable water resistance; it is not equivalent to a waterproof trouser.
