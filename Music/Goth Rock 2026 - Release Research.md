# Goth Rock — 2026 Release Research

Checked **14 November 2026** for [[After the Last Light - Goth Rock 2026|After the Last Light]]. Thirty releases by thirty artists were checked directly against their artist or label Bandcamp pages. All selected editions are available, have at least three tracks and are dated within the 2026 boundary. Covers come from those release pages; the original artists and rights holders retain ownership.

## Selection and genre evidence

Only new multi-track releases are included. A newly recorded album containing covers is eligible; a reissue of a pre-2026 album is not. Descriptions paraphrase release notes and credits rather than pretending to be listening reviews. Suggested starting tracks are editorial choices, checked against the published track lists.

Genre claims follow release-specific artist tags or album descriptions. Label-wide tags alone are insufficient: for example, Omelas is linked to darkwave and post-punk because its description names them, not to deathrock because the label carries that tag. Artist biography statements are identified as broader project descriptions. No metal-to-goth bridge has been inferred from a shared dark aesthetic.

## Date and edition notes

- **Black Doldrums:** the release page explicitly corrects the earlier 13 October listing to **13 November 2026**.
- **Normal Weirds:** the artist Bandcamp edition is dated **2 October 2026**; the Bat-Cave edition is dated **9 November**. The issue uses the artist edition.
- **Forever Grey:** **13 November 2026** is the digital release. December physical shipping dates do not move it outside this issue.
- **Denuit:** the original title is *nothing lasts | forever*. Navigation uses a slash and the filename uses a hyphen because the vertical bar is reserved in wikilinks and Windows filenames. The original title is retained in the album note.
- **Caput Medusae / Daniel Knutz:** storefront suffixes “[Album]” and “(Album)” are omitted from navigation; the original storefront titles remain in the album notes.
- **Locations:** artist account locations are used only on artist pages; label account locations are not assigned to artists. Edition labels are named only when supported by the release page. “Artist Bandcamp edition” does not assert that no label exists.
