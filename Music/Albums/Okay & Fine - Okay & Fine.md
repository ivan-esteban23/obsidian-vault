# Okay & Fine — Okay & Fine

![[attachments/Indie 2026/22-okay-fine.jpg|320]]

**20 August 2026** · Album · Indie folk pop / home recording · Artist Bandcamp edition

Kirsten Elfe builds a debut around wordplay, ukulele and experiences of longing and mental health. Her father Charles adds keyboards and bongos. Recorded in their shared living room, the album moves between whimsical touches and more vulnerable writing across ten songs.

**First spin:** Let Me Stay a Concept.
