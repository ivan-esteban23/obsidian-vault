# Rosetta Stone — Executing Nostalgia

![[attachments/Goth Rock 2026/04-rosetta-stone.jpg|320]]

**25 September 2026** · Album · Gothic rock / darkwave · Cleopatra Records

Porl King's Rosetta Stone pairs new original songs with newly recorded covers on this studio album. Thunder Bae guests on Incubus Succubus, while the running order moves from Shadowban towards revisited underground favorites. The mixture is part of the album's concept; this is a 2026 studio release rather than a vintage-album reissue.
