# Merino vs Synthetic Base Layers

Base-layer selection balances drying, odor, abrasion, skin comfort and care. Fiber content is a starting point; knit, weight and blend also affect performance.

## How to decide

Merino can be attractive for repeated wear and next-to-skin feel, while synthetic constructions can be useful when frequent high-output wetting and drying dominate. Blends can trade between these priorities. Do not assign one absolute drying or warmth advantage to every garment carrying the same fiber label. [Base-layer selection](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/underwear.html).

## Put it into practice

Test the chosen fabric under the actual shoulder straps. A lightweight merino layer may need gentler handling; a synthetic top may need more frequent cleaning for acceptable odor. Select weight for effort and use separate insulation for colder stops.

## Failure to avoid

Common mistake: assuming wool remains fully warm when saturated, or that a thick base is automatically appropriate because the destination is Patagonia.
