# Down vs Synthetic Insulation

Insulation selection depends on warmth, volume, moisture exposure and the chances of drying. Neither fill type is universally best for Patagonia or every winter route.

## How to decide

Down can provide useful warmth at low packed bulk when the complete garment or sleeping system is appropriate and kept dry. Synthetic insulation can be a practical choice for repeated damp use, but it is not immune to water, compression or wear. Compare construction and fill quantity rather than the material label alone. [Backpacking sleeping bags](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/sleeping-bag-backpacking.html).

## Put it into practice

For a wet multi-day trek, first improve the dry-storage system. Then decide whether synthetic stop clothing and protected down sleep insulation provide a useful balance. In dry cold, down may be attractive, but perspiration and snow entry still need management.

## Failure to avoid

Common mistake: comparing fill-power numbers as if they directly described jacket warmth, or interpreting hydrophobic treatment as waterproofness.
