# Backpacking

## Activity demands

Self-supported multi-day walking adds shelter, sleep equipment, cooking and water weight to the clothing problem. Dampness and friction accumulate over days.

## Layering strategy

Split the kit into a moving outfit and a dry sleeping/stop reserve. Size shell and insulation to allow movement beneath shoulder straps and a hipbelt. [Layering functions and exertion](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/layering-basics.html).

## Gear decisions

Test a loaded pack, a complete sleep system and a wet-weather camp routine. Choose shelter architecture for exposure and maintain a dry barrier around critical insulation.

## Common mistakes

Optimizing jacket weight while ignoring shelter or pack load; storing dry clothes with wet rainwear; assuming every evening allows drying.
