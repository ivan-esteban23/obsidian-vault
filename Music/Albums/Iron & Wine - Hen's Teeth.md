# Iron & Wine — Hen's Teeth

![[attachments/Indie 2026/19-iron-wine.jpg|320]]

**27 February 2026** · Album · Folk / folk rock / ensemble songwriting · Sub Pop Records

Sam Beam's eighth full-length shares sessions and musicians with Light Verse while taking its own course. The release notes describe experimentation with genre, including folk-rock songs that expand towards larger endings. I'm With Her appears twice, adding another vocal presence to this group-centered record.

**First spin:** Roses.
