# ALL NOW — The Beautiful Living & The Glorious Dead

![[attachments/Alternative 2026/08-all-now.jpg|320]]

**17 July 2026** · Mini-album · Alternative rock / emo / pop-punk · Artist Bandcamp edition

Manchester's ALL NOW packs six songs into a mini-album concerned with returning to music after accumulating more life experience. The artist describes classic emo and pop-punk urgency within a reflective perspective. Meet Me At Victoria opens a set that closes simply with An Ending.
