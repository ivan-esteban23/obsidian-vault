# La Mano Sinistra — Omelas

![[attachments/Goth Rock 2026/29-la-mano-sinistra.jpg|320]]

**6 November 2026** · Album · Post-punk / darkwave · Bat-Cave Productions

Born from a theatrical production, Omelas adapts Ursula K. Le Guin's ethical dilemma into a seven-track concept album. The label explicitly describes darkwave textures and post-punk rhythms, with a theatrical core. Unnecessary Perfection introduces its question of whether a society's apparent happiness can justify the suffering concealed beneath it.

**First spin:** Unnecessary Perfection.
