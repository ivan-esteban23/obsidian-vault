# Static Insulation

**Function:** Reduce heat loss when stopping, waiting or spending time at camp.

## Selection criteria

Compare total construction, fill quantity, coverage and fit, not fill power alone. Allow space for loft over the intended layers. Synthetic fill can be a practical choice where drying is unreliable; protected down can reduce packed bulk. [Backpacking sleeping bags](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/sleeping-bag-backpacking.html).

## Use and field checks

Store in a waterproof inner barrier. Put it on before a stop becomes uncomfortable and protect it from rain, cooking moisture and tent contact.

## Failure modes and tradeoffs

A warm jacket compressed beneath an undersized shell loses useful loft; wet insulation can lose performance.
