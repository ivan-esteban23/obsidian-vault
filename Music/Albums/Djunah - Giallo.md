# Djunah — Giallo

![[attachments/Alternative 2026/23-djunah.jpg|320]]

**30 October 2026** · Album · Noise rock / experimental arrangements · Artist Bandcamp edition

Donna Diane writes and performs a noise-rock opera about complex trauma, drawing on Italian horror cinema. A horn section joins the record, with Kurt Ballou and Brian Deck among the recording credits. Rotting Age offers an entrance into its twelve-part sequence.
