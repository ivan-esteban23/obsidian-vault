# Water Treatment

**Function:** Match a treatment method to the route’s water hazards and the equipment’s documented capability.

## Selection criteria

Distinguish filters from purifiers and check pathogen claims, contact times and maintenance in the manufacturer instructions. Carry enough capacity for the longest credible dry section. [Backcountry water treatment](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/water-treatment-backcountry.html).

## Use and field checks

Protect freeze-sensitive equipment and keep clean and untreated containers distinct. A backup method helps when a primary system fails or clogs.

## Failure modes and tradeoffs

Clear-looking water is not proof of safety. A small filter does not create water on an arid section or remove every chemical contaminant.
