# Torres del Paine

Torres del Paine is the collection’s main multi-day trekking case study. The official visitor guidance emphasizes rapidly changing weather and clothing for rain, wind and snow. [Torres del Paine official 2026 recommendations](https://parquetorresdelpaine.cl/recomendaciones-para-su-viaje-2026/).

## Planning context

Use a W or O itinerary only with current route, reservation and access information. The official 2026–2027 bulletin requires advance accommodation arrangements for these circuits; operating details and weather restrictions must be rechecked before travel.

## Clothing and layering

Start with a light base, adjustable moving insulation, controlled hood and cuffs, reliable rain coverage and a protected stop layer. Add hand and head protection that can be reached without unpacking the sleep reserve. In wet conditions, separate the rain shell from a dry camp jacket.

## Multi-day equipment priorities

The overnight system deserves equal attention: a suitable fully supported shelter, mat and bag considered together, independent waterproof packing, tested footwear and a realistic food/fuel plan. Follow designated camping and cooking arrangements. Download maps; do not rely on interior mobile coverage for navigation or communication.
