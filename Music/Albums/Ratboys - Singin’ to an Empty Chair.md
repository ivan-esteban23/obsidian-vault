# Ratboys — Singin’ to an Empty Chair

![[attachments/Indie 2026/01-ratboys.jpg|320]]

**6 February 2026** · Album · Indie rock / post-country · New West Records

Julia Steiner writes into the space left by an estranged loved one on Ratboys' sixth album. Chris Walla returns as co-producer, with sessions moving from a Wisconsin cabin to Chicago-area studios. The release notes identify Anywhere as a power-pop turn and Penny in the Lake as post-country.

**First spin:** Anywhere.
