# Multi-Day Trekking

For trips where clothing, food, shelter, sleep equipment and repeated wetting must remain manageable over several consecutive days. The main test is system reliability, not the lightest individual item.

## Functional kit

| Phase | Functions to cover |
| --- | --- |
| Moving outfit | Base, adaptable legwear, suitable footwear and the lightest insulation needed for the effort. |
| Weather reserve | Accessible shell, rain legwear where warranted, gloves and head coverage. |
| Protected reserve | Dry stop/sleep clothing, appropriate bag or quilt, insulated mat and a reliable packing barrier. |

## Field sequence

1. Test the pack with the full load, including food, water and the largest expected insulation combination.
2. Separate wet shelter/rainwear from sleep equipment every time the pack is reorganized.
3. Plan food, water, fuel, campsites and communication together; none is solved by a clothing brand.
4. At each camp, inspect dampness and damage, protect the sleep area and prepare the next morning’s accessible layers.

## Failure to avoid

A spare garment is not truly a dry reserve if it shares the same leaking packing space. Do not rely on a sunny lunch break to recover saturated sleep insulation.
