# Wind Shells

**Function:** Reduce wind-driven heat loss during relatively dry movement with less bulk than many storm shells.

## Selection criteria

Compare air permeability, hood control, cuff adjustment and actual rain limitations. Softshells and light woven windshirts serve different abrasion and warmth needs. [Rainwear selection](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/rainwear.html).

## Use and field checks

Use over a light base or fleece when exposed. Keep a waterproof shell available when the forecast calls for sustained rain. In high wind, secure hem and hood before leaving shelter.

## Failure modes and tradeoffs

DWR and a close weave do not establish sustained waterproofness.
