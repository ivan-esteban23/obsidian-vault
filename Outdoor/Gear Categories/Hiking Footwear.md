# Hiking Footwear

**Function:** Connect foot fit, terrain, load and weather; the best brand is the one whose appropriate model fits the actual use.

## Selection criteria

Test both feet with trekking socks and a realistic pack. Check heel hold, toe clearance on descent and width after feet have warmed up. Select traction, stiffness and waterproof construction for the route. [Hiking footwear selection and fit](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/hiking-boots.html).

## Use and field checks

Break in boots that need it, inspect soles and carry a workable foot-care plan. Verify crampon compatibility separately for technical routes.

## Failure modes and tradeoffs

Waterproof footwear can fill from above and dry slowly. A high cuff alone is not a guarantee against injury.
