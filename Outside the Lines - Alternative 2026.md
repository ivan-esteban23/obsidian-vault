# Outside the Lines

*Alternative · 2026*

**Thirty releases. Thirty artists. Four listening routes.**

Alternative guitar music moves from melodic hooks into rougher edges, noise and expansive textures. This selection joins established bands and underground discoveries, giving each record its own feature and a source-backed account of its connections.
