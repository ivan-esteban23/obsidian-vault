# Apokalipstick — Gaunt

**8 March 2026** · EP · Deathrock / goth-punk · Bat-Cave Productions

Three songs introduce Apokalipstick, a deathrock act from Wrocław. The label places this debut EP in an anarchist, punk-minded world of apocalyptic ugliness. American Nightmare, Cockroach and the title track keep the feature compact: a short first encounter with a project that later expanded its catalogue during the same year.
