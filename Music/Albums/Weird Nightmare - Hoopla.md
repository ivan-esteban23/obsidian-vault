# Weird Nightmare — Hoopla

![[attachments/Alternative 2026/05-weird-nightmare.jpg|320]]

**1 May 2026** · Album · Power pop / indie rock / fuzzy guitar pop · Sub Pop Records

Alex Edkins turns further towards concise, melodic songwriting on Weird Nightmare's second album. Co-produced with Spoon's Jim Eno, the record foregrounds power-pop hooks and guitar crunch. It provides this issue with a brighter guitar-pop entry point; Edkins' former band METZ supplies background rather than an album-level noise-rock label.

**First spin:** Headful of Rain.
