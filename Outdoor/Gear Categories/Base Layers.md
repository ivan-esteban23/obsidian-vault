# Base Layers

**Function:** Manage next-to-skin moisture and comfort; base layers are not the main wind or rain barrier.

## Selection criteria

Choose weight for effort first. A light layer plus adjustable insulation is often more versatile than a very warm base that cannot be removed easily. Compare merino, synthetic and blends for drying, odor, abrasion and skin comfort. [Layering functions and exertion](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/layering-basics.html).

## Use and field checks

Try the garment beneath pack straps. Keep an uncontaminated dry reserve for sleeping on multi-day trips. Avoid assuming the word thermal specifies a temperature limit.

## Failure modes and tradeoffs

Fine wool can abrade and any material can remain damp if the system cannot release sweat.
