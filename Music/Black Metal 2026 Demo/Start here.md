# Explore your black metal collection

Try links, Canvas, and Graph view with six records from your magazine.

## 1. Follow a link

Open [[Music/Black Metal 2026 Demo/Albums/Misotheist - De Pinte|Misotheist - De Pinte]], then follow its label link. In the label note, open **Backlinks** from the right sidebar to see which notes reference it. Ctrl-hover a note link to preview it.

## 2. Explore the Canvas

Open [[Music/Black Metal 2026 Demo/Black Metal Explorer.canvas|Black Metal Explorer]]. Drag a card, zoom with the mouse wheel, and double-click a note card to edit it. Click an album title to open its companion note, or follow the label beneath it. Each tile connects an album to its label. The three style links in the header open the browsing notes.

Canvas lines are manually drawn relationships. Graph lines come from links written inside notes. Links in Canvas text-only cards do not create note backlinks.

## 3. Explore the graph

Open Graph view from the left ribbon. In its filter, paste:

```text
path:"Music/Black Metal 2026 Demo" -file:"Start here"
```

Turn **Attachments** off. The prepared graph tab uses album, label, and style colors. Click an album node, then use the command palette to open **Local graph**. Set its depth to 2 to explore nearby connections. Open **Backlinks** to see incoming references.
