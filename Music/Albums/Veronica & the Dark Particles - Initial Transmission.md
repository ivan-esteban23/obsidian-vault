# Veronica & the Dark Particles — Initial Transmission

![[attachments/Goth Rock 2026/18-veronica-the-dark-particles.jpg|320]]

**28 August 2026** · Album · Darkwave / coldwave · Artist Bandcamp edition

The duo introduces its debut as galactic darkwave, pairing coldwave bass patterns and post-punk melancholy with club-oriented production. Liquify opens an eight-track sequence that also revisits The Cure's The Kiss. Synth textures and Veronica Blak's treated voice supply the record's stated tech-noir setting, with Enic Nine sharing writing and production duties.

**First spin:** Liquify.
