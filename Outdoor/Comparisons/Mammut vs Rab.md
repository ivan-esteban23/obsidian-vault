# Mammut vs Rab

Mammut offers a broad alpine equipment perspective; Rab supplies a strong insulation and clothing-system comparison with detailed Material Facts. Breadth and specialization are different advantages.

## Comparison framework

| Decision | First perspective | Second perspective |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary starting point | Mammut: clothing, footwear and carrying equipment for mountain use. | Rab: mountain insulation, sleep equipment and protective clothing. |
| Technical evidence | Repair-and-care documentation supports a longevity discussion. | Material Facts supports component-level material comparison and states its omissions. |
| Cold overnight trip | Compare the actual insulation, fit and sleep equipment. | Compare on the same basis; fill power or a material family alone is not a warmth rating. |

## What to choose for Chile

Start with Mammut when several alpine equipment categories need consideration, and Rab when insulation and sleep equipment drive the decision. Either route still benefits from independently fitting footwear and a balanced wet-weather plan.

## What this comparison cannot establish

Whole-brand ratings are editorial, within-specialty judgments. They do not establish a measurable difference between two specific garments, a universal fit, or service eligibility in Chile. Product version, construction and fit should decide a close purchase.
