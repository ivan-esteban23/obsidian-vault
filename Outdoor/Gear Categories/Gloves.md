# Gloves

**Function:** Preserve hand comfort and the ability to operate equipment in exposed conditions.

## Selection criteria

Compare liner, insulating and weather-protective roles. Check palm durability, cuff overlap and whether a spare dry pair is more useful than one very thick pair. [Layering functions and exertion](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/layering-basics.html).

## Use and field checks

Open zippers, use poles, adjust guylines and handle the navigation device while wearing them. Keep spare hand protection in the dry reserve.

## Failure modes and tradeoffs

A waterproof insert does not stop water entering through the cuff; tight gloves restrict usable insulation and dexterity.
