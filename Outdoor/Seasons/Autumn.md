# Autumn

**Southern Hemisphere default:** March–May. These are meteorological planning months, not guaranteed snow, rain or park-opening dates. Northern Hemisphere use requires shifting the calendar and checking the local climate.

## Environmental characteristics

Shortening days reduce travel and drying time. Wetter conditions and colder rests can make accumulated moisture more important over consecutive days. For Chile, distinguish [[Outdoor/Regional Guides/Central Chile Mediterranean Climate|Central Chile Mediterranean Climate]] from [[Outdoor/Regional Guides/Patagonia Region|Patagonia Region]] and the [[Outdoor/Regional Guides/Chilean Andes|Chilean Andes]]. [Chile national climate communication, 2016](https://mma.gob.cl/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/TCN-2016b1.pdf).

## Layering recommendations

Favor a clear separation between moving layers and dry camp clothing. Add rain legwear and more stop insulation when the forecast, elevation and trip length justify them. See [[Outdoor/Layering Systems/Moving vs Static Insulation|Moving vs Static Insulation]] for the transition between effort and rest.

## Common mistakes

Planning summer daily distances without daylight allowance; relying on drying a sleeping bag during lunch; carrying only one damp base layer.
