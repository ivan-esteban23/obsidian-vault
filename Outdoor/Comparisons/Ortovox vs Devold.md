# Ortovox vs Devold

Devold is principally a wool-base-layer comparison; Ortovox extends wool into a wider mountain clothing and insulation system. Compare equivalent garment roles before comparing whole catalogues.

## Comparison framework

| Decision | First perspective | Second perspective |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base layer | Devold offers different weights including expedition-oriented constructions. | Ortovox places merino within its mountain clothing system. |
| Insulation | A thick base layer is not a complete weather or stop system. | Swisswool and newer insulation concepts are separate product constructions. |
| Responsibility evidence | Sheep to Shop and supply-chain visibility are useful evidence. | Check the current RWS sourcing disclosure rather than assuming the older OWP label is unchanged. |

## What to choose for Chile

Choose a light base for high effort and add insulation independently. For multi-day Chilean trekking, evaluate drying, pack-strap abrasion and comfort rather than assuming a wool-centric brand makes every layer the best option.

## What this comparison cannot establish

Whole-brand ratings are editorial, within-specialty judgments. They do not establish a measurable difference between two specific garments, a universal fit, or service eligibility in Chile. Product version, construction and fit should decide a close purchase.
