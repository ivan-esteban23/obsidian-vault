# Sub-Arctic

**Planning context:** Long cold seasons, a short growing season, exposed uplands and variable ground conditions form a broad northern planning context. Wet ground and summer insects can coexist with cold nights. Climate labels here overlap: a maritime route can also be wet cold, and an alpine route can cross Mediterranean foothills.

## Weather characteristics

Long cold seasons, a short growing season, exposed uplands and variable ground conditions form a broad northern planning context. Wet ground and summer insects can coexist with cold nights.

## Main risks

Overgeneralizing a northern label into one temperature range, insufficient wind protection, wet crossings and inadequate reserves far from easy drying facilities.

## Recommended layering strategy

Combine maintainable trekking layers with wind and rain reserves. Scale stop insulation and the sleep system to the actual forecast and latitude; summer does not automatically need expedition-weight base layers. This is a function-based starting point; no fixed temperature limit is inferred from a brand or fabric name. [Layering functions and exertion](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/layering-basics.html).

## Seasonal considerations

Summer may need insect coverage and wet-ground planning. Winter demands a substantially different warmth and daylight strategy.
