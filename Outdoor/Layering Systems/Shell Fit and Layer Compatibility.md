# Shell Fit and Layer Compatibility

A clothing system needs enough space and articulation for movement and insulation without openings that cannot be controlled in wind.

## How to decide

Try the shell over the layers intended for the worst credible conditions. Raise both arms, reach forward, squat and tighten the loaded hipbelt. Confirm that cuffs overlap, the hem remains useful and the hood turns with the head. Inspect whether straps block vents or pockets. [Layering functions and exertion](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/layering-basics.html).

## Put it into practice

Repeat the test with gloves. Decide where static insulation sits in the sequence and whether it can retain loft. Check trouser mobility and footwear fit separately; a jacket size is not a complete fit system.

## Failure to avoid

Common mistake: using a universal claim that Scandinavian clothing is long or European clothing is narrow. Brand profiles give only cautious tendencies; product dimensions and the wearer’s trial determine fit.
