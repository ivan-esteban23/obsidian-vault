# Emergency Communication

**Function:** Provide a way to raise an alert where ordinary phone coverage is absent or unreliable.

## Selection criteria

Evaluate service area, subscription, device limitations and the people who will receive trip information. A satellite messenger, beacon and mobile phone have different functions. [Ten Essentials](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/ten-essentials.html).

## Use and field checks

Test allowed non-emergency functions before leaving and give a responsible contact an itinerary and overdue plan. Keep the device accessible and powered.

## Failure modes and tradeoffs

Communication is not immediate rescue or a substitute for a turnaround decision. No device brand or Chilean service contract has been verified in this collection.

## Brand candidates

No specialist manufacturer for this category is evaluated in the 25-brand set. Select using the criteria above and verified product documentation; an unrelated clothing brand is not substituted merely to fill a matrix.
