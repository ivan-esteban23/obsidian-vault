# Rab

**Country:** United Kingdom · **Origin:** United Kingdom · **Region:** European · **Cost:** Premium. Country identifies the brand base used here, not where every product is manufactured.

## Brand philosophy

Mountain insulation and protective clothing are supported by detailed product-level material information. [Rab — design / technical evidence](https://rab.equipment/uk/rab-lab/are-down-jackets-waterproof).

## Strengths

A strong candidate when camp insulation, sleeping equipment and a repairable clothing system need to work together. Material Facts helps compare components rather than slogans.

## Weaknesses and tradeoffs

Pertex Quantum is a lightweight fabric family, not equivalent to a waterproof Pertex Shield construction. Lightweight down still needs abrasion and moisture management. These are editorial selection tradeoffs, not measured defect rates.
