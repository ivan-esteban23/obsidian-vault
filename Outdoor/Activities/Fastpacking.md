# Fastpacking

## Activity demands

A faster run/walk itinerary reduces some equipment weight but preserves the need for an exposed stop and a credible overnight plan. Pack movement and sweat load become especially important.

## Layering strategy

Use a light base and a highly adjustable weather system. Keep emergency warmth rather than assuming continuous movement will keep you warm. [Layering functions and exertion](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/layering-basics.html).

## Gear decisions

Test footwear and pack stability at the actual pace. A minimal shelter is appropriate only when exposure, campsites and user competence support it.

## Common mistakes

Removing the stop layer to hit a target weight; choosing a windshirt for an extended rain forecast; planning no margin for a slower day.
