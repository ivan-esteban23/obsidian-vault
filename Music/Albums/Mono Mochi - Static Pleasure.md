**11 September 2026** · Album · Dream pop / indie rock / indie pop · Artist Bandcamp edition

Lucerne's Mono Mochi builds its second album from ideas developed together in a mountain cabin and rehearsal room. The release notes stress a handmade recording process and describe stories of repetition, romance and self-sabotage. Fields At Dawn supplies a road-trip starting point within the eleven tracks.
