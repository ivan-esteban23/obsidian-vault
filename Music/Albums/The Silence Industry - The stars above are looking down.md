# The Silence Industry — The stars above are looking down

![[attachments/Goth Rock 2026/06-the-silence-industry.jpg|320]]

**13 March 2026** · Album · Industrial goth / gothic rock / post-rock · Aenaos Records

The Silence Industry builds its fourteenth long-form release from delay-heavy guitars, drum machines, bass and noise experiments. Five original compositions sit beside a traditional-song arrangement and shorter experiments. The artist frames the record around anxiety, mourning and resistance, with hand-drawn illustrations and a printable zine extending its DIY approach.

**First spin:** Shadows on your throne.
