# Mitski — Nothing's About to Happen to Me

![[attachments/Indie 2026/02-mitski.jpg|320]]

**27 February 2026** · Album · Rock / orchestral songwriting · Dead Oceans

Mitski reunites her touring band for an eighth studio album built with live ensemble playing and orchestral arrangements. Patrick Hyland produces, while Drew Erickson arranges and conducts the orchestra. Where's My Phone? is one of the singles highlighted on the artist's own release page.
